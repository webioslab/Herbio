01 / ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Understand the brain.
Build what comes next.

A collaborative learning experience at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence.

The summer school brings together neuroscience, AI and machine learning, brain-signal processing, biosensing, and wearable technologies. Participants connect foundational concepts with practical experiments and team-based research challenges.

05 / PARTNERS & FUNDING

Connecting Egypt and the United Kingdom

A cross-institutional partnership connecting research, education, and community.

The HERBioLab project is funded by the British Council under the UK–Egypt Higher Education Partnerships Programme.

It is implemented through a collaboration between WeBios Lab, Department of Electronics and Communications Engineering, College of Engineering and Technology, the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT), Main Campus, Alexandria, Egypt, and Aston University, Birmingham, United Kingdom.
